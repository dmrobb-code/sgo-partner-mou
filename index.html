Roles & responsibilities

Who does what

Our commitment is to shoulder as much of the administrative burden as possible so you can stay focused on educating students.

Bluum SGO will

  • Provide a secure digital application and procurement portal (powered by Odyssey)
  • Verify student income eligibility and apply the Program’s priority requirements
  • Pay qualified educational expenses directly to the education provider
  • Collect donations and issue official tax acknowledgments
  • Supply customizable marketing materials, toolkits and template communications
  • Handle reporting, accounting, recordkeeping and audit requirements

Your school or district will

  • Inform patrons, board members, alumni and community members about the opportunity
  • Use existing channels such as newsletters, email and social media to share materials
  • Identify offerings qualified students may fund with scholarships
  • Verify enrollment and document how scholarship funds are spent
  • Name a primary administrative liaison
  • Share feedback to help improve the Program

A note from Bluum SGO

Dear Idaho Education Leaders

We are eager to partner with you to generate additional resources to support students and families in your community. The Federal Scholarship Tax Credit offers tremendous potential to fund learning opportunities that are not currently funded in Idaho.

This new program will require all of us to think and work differently. It is a learning process for everyone involved, and our commitment to you is to carry as much of that burden as we can.

That is why we developed this MOU. It helps ensure we are on the same page from the start. Despite its length and wordiness, it is not legally binding in the ways that matter most: you are free to walk away if you believe we are not holding up our end of the bargain.

This is an exciting opportunity to bring new resources to Idaho’s students and families, and it is a privilege to do this work alongside you.

Sincerely,
Duncan RobbExecutive Director, Bluum Scholarship Granting Organization, Inc.
